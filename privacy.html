Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

"Eike Pipeline" is a private tool used only by Eike Oyang to upload, schedule and analyse videos on his own channels (YouTube, Gan Jing World, Instagram). It is not offered to other users.

  • Data accessed: only Eike Oyang's own channel data (videos, metadata, playlists, captions, comments and analytics) via the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and the official APIs of the other platforms.
  • Use: solely to publish Eike's own videos and to create performance reports for himself.
  • Storage: access tokens and reports are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Third parties: no personal data of other people is collected, apart from publicly visible comments on Eike's own videos, which are read only to draft replies.
  • Revoking access: at any time via Google account permissions.
  • This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Contact: via youtube.com/@eike_oyang.
